-- Personal Tracker System

-- Overview

Personal Tracker is an AI-powered career development platform that helps students and job seekers understand what skills they need for their desired career.

Instead of simply suggesting courses, the platform compares current market requirements with the user's existing skills and creates a personalized roadmap.

The system also tracks learning progress and prepares users for interviews.

---

-- Problem Statement

Many students face these problems:

- They don't know which skills companies currently require.
- They don't know whether their resume is good enough.
- They don't know what to learn next.
- They lose track of their progress.
- They struggle with interview preparation.

This project solves these problems in one platform.
